Making a Data-Driven Career Decision

Quitting a job is an emotional decision, but letting emotions alone drive it can lead to regret. Evaluating tangible metrics — your financial runway, burnout level, salary fairness, and growth trajectory — helps clarify whether you are running toward something better or just running away from a temporary rough patch.

Four Key Career Pillars

We evaluate Financial Security (how long can you survive without this job), Workplace Mental Health (is the environment toxic or just stressful), Salary Fairness (are you paid market rate), and Career Trajectory (is this role helping you grow or holding you back). Each pillar is scored, and the overall picture tells you whether staying, leaving, or negotiating makes the most sense.

The Financial Runway Question

Before quitting without another job lined up, you need a realistic runway. The rule of thumb is 3 to 6 months of living expenses saved, but that depends on your industry's hiring speed, your seniority level, and the current job market. The tool helps you calculate your actual safe quit date based on your specific numbers rather than generic advice.

Beyond the Score

A low score does not mean you must quit tomorrow. Use it as a signal to start updating your resume, networking, and exploring options while you still have the security of a paycheck. The best time to look for a job is when you do not desperately need one — and this tool helps you recognize that moment before a crisis forces your hand.
