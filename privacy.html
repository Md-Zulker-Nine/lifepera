Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026 · Effective: June 10, 2026 · lifepera.com

The Short Version

  • Tool answers and calculations are handled in your browser; the site has no tool-submission endpoint.
  • Google Analytics 4 and Google AdSense are loaded only if you choose “Accept all” in the consent banner; each service has its own privacy practices.
  • Your consent choice is stored in your browser's local storage.
  • You can contact us at contact@lifepera.com with any privacy questions.

1. Who We Are

LifePera ("we," "us," or "our") operates the website lifepera.com as an online service for a global audience. This site does not publish a business street address or claim a particular operating location. Our contact email is contact@lifepera.com.

2. Tool Inputs

All interactive tools on LifePera run in your browser using JavaScript, and the site has no tool-submission endpoint. The tool code does not send your answers or results to LifePera's servers. If you choose “Accept all,” Google Analytics and AdSense scripts run and may process browser or page-use data as described below.

  • Your income figures entered into the How Rich Am I tool
  • Your quiz answers in the Attachment Style, EQ, or personality quizzes
  • Your relationship checklist responses in the Toxic Relationship tool
  • Your email text entered into the Email Tone Checker
  • Any other personal information entered into any tool on this website

3. Information We Do Collect

Contact messages: The contact page opens your email application to send your name, email address, subject, and message. If you email us directly, we receive the information you choose to include in that message.

Analytics data: If you choose “Accept all,” we load Google Analytics 4 to understand how visitors use the site. Google Analytics may process information such as pages visited, device and browser details, and approximate location. It is not loaded before that choice. See Google’s privacy policy for details of Google’s processing.

4. Advertising — Google AdSense

If you choose “Accept all,” the site loads Google AdSense. Google may use cookies and similar technologies to provide, measure, and protect advertising. Loading the script does not guarantee that an ad will be shown; ad delivery depends on Google’s approval and configuration. If you choose “Only essential” or “Reject optional,” the AdSense script is not loaded. Learn more at Google’s advertising policy.

Opt-out: You can opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google Ad Settings. You can also opt out of third-party cookies used for interest-based advertising through the Network Advertising Initiative opt-out page.

5. Cookies and Browser Storage

LifePera stores your consent choice in browser local storage. Optional analytics and advertising scripts may use cookies or similar technologies only after you accept all:

  • Preference storage: The site uses local storage in your browser to remember your consent choice; it does not use a first-party preference cookie.
  • Analytics: Google Analytics 4 is loaded only after you choose “Accept all.” Google may then use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies.
  • Advertising: Google AdSense is loaded only after you choose “Accept all.” Google may then use cookies or similar technologies under its own policies.

You can reopen the consent banner with the “Privacy settings” button at any time. Changing from “Accept all” reloads the page so optional scripts stop loading. This cannot undo information already sent or remove cookies controlled by third parties; use your browser settings and Google Ad Settings to manage those separately. Removing this site’s local-storage preference in your browser will show the banner again.

6. Privacy Questions and Requests

For questions about this policy or information you sent by email, contact contact@lifepera.com. This page describes the site's current observable behavior and does not claim compliance with a particular privacy law.

7. Data Retention

The site has no account system, tool-result database, or tool-submission endpoint. Email correspondence is handled through the email service used to contact us. If enabled by your consent, analytics data is retained according to the retention settings of Google Analytics.

8. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices of these sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies before providing any personal information.

9. Children's Privacy

LifePera is not designed for children under 13 and has no account system or tool-submission endpoint. For questions about information sent to us by email, contact contact@lifepera.com.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last updated" date. We encourage you to review this page periodically.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us at:

Email: contact@lifepera.com
Website: lifepera.com
